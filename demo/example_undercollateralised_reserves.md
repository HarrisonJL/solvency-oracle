# Example Stablecoin (YUSD) - Reserve Attestation

This is a demo attestation page for the [SolvencyOracle](https://github.com/HarrisonJL/solvency-oracle) Intelligent Contract - not a real financial instrument.

As of this report, Example Stablecoin (YUSD) holds total reserves of $70,000,000, consisting of cash and short-term commercial paper.

Total circulating supply (liabilities) is $100,000,000 YUSD tokens outstanding, each redeemable 1:1 for US dollars.
