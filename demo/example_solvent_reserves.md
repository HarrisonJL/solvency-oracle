# Example Stablecoin (XUSD) - Reserve Attestation

This is a demo attestation page for the [SolvencyOracle](https://github.com/HarrisonJL/solvency-oracle) Intelligent Contract - not a real financial instrument.

As of this report, Example Stablecoin (XUSD) holds total reserves of $125,000,000, consisting of cash and short-term US Treasury securities held with regulated custodians.

Total circulating supply (liabilities) is $100,000,000 XUSD tokens outstanding, each redeemable 1:1 for US dollars.
